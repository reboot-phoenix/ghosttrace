About GhostTrace

GhostTrace is a free tool that checks whether a link, phone number, UPI ID, or email looks like a scam. Paste what you received, and it gives you a plain-language verdict with the evidence behind it, so you can decide whether to trust it.

Who built this

GhostTrace is built by a cybersecurity student in Kolkata, India, as a personal and academic project. It is not affiliated with any bank, government body, or company named in its results.

How it decides

It checks against public lists of known scam and phishing sites, looks for patterns common in scam links and messages (urgent language, brand impersonation, requests for OTP or card details), and — once enough people use it — against reports other users have submitted. Every verdict shows its reasons; it never just says "trust me."

It is not perfect. In testing against a large sample of real, already-reported phishing sites, the automated checks alone caught roughly 1 in 4 that weren't yet on any known-bad list. A "no strong signals" result means nothing suspicious was found — not that something is guaranteed safe. Always verify independently, especially before sending money.

If you've been scammed

GhostTrace cannot get your money back or investigate a crime. If you've lost money or shared sensitive details:

  • Report immediately at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930 (India's cyber-fraud helpline) — speed matters for recovering funds.
  • Contact your bank's fraud department directly.

Feedback and reports

Every result has buttons to confirm or correct the verdict, and to report something as a scam. These help improve the checks over time — see the project README for how that works.
